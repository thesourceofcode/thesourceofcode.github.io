# The Bulletin of the National Retail Dry Goods Association 1946-05, page 46

## New Methods

The addressograph sales recorder is a portable, lightweight register which is loaded with fifty sales slip forms in continuous strip, flat pack. Such forms can be single, two part, three part, four part, or five part forms. The Addressograph system includes providing each store customer with an embossed metal plate carrying name, address, account number, etc.

The customer presents this plate to the clerk who inserts it into the recorder device. With one stroke of the machine, the clerk can imprint the sales slip not only with the data embossed on the customer plate but also with all other pertinent information such as department number, clerk numr, date, kind of sale, and other auxiliary data, such as item purchased. This sales re¬ corder is the most recent development presented to retail stores to insure speed, accuracy and economy in writing sales slips.

Whether they relate to charge sales or cash sales, the advantage and benefits of this device will reflect themselves not only in more speech handling of the customer at the time of the purchase, but also in every division of the business.
