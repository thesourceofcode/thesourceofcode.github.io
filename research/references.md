Credit cards issue din the country can be classified into two categories
1. Cards issued to indian nationals who hold a blanket foreign exchange permit
2. card issued for use within the country

customer signs copy of the bill which merchant sends to issuing agency which is ultimately sent to the holder to make the payment

Crucial datapoints:
- ALmost all banks take an annual service charge from the user
- some banks charge an entrance fee for membership
- usual billing cycle upto one month
- credit facility on "revolving terms" available
- most credit cards allow cas withdrawal a few times every month
- misuse of cards is a major problem and the most common malpractice is forgery. signature on the card largely the only
mode of identification of the card holder, it is often impossible to place a forgery if it is close to the original
