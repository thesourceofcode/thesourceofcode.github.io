# Buffalo Industrial Bank — Newspaper Advertisements: Transcriptions & English Translations

> **Note on the bank's name:** All five advertisements are for the **Buffalo Industrial Bank** of Buffalo, N.Y. — *not* "Buffalo International Bank." The name "BUFFALO INDUSTRIAL BANK" appears clearly in every ad. This file uses the name as printed.

## Source

All ads are from the same newspaper:

- **Newspaper:** *Dziennik Dla Wszystkich* ("Everybody's Daily") — Buffalo, N.Y.'s Polish-language daily newspaper.
- **Language of ads:** Polish (Buffalo Polish-American dialect, with English banking terms left untranslated in the original copy).
- **Advertiser:** Buffalo Industrial Bank (an affiliate of Great Lakes Commercial Corporation), Buffalo, N.Y. Member FDIC.
- **Bank locations referenced across the ads:**
  - **Main office:** Washington & Huron Sts. (1945) → moved to **Court & Pearl Sts.** (from Monday, April 5, 1948)
  - **East Side branch:** 694 Fillmore Ave. at Broadway
  - **South Side branch:** 211 Cazenovia St. at Seneca
  - **Telephone (from 1951):** WA. 4950
- **Recurring brand:** **BANKWAY** — the bank's installment-financing plan/credit card, promoted with the tagline *"Buy on time the 'BANKWAY' — Ask your dealer!"* and an "Approved Dealer" logo.

## Recurring elements & vocabulary notes

- **"Członek Federal Deposit Insurance Corporation"** = "Member Federal Deposit Insurance Corporation" (FDIC). The 1952 ad misprints it as "Federal Insurance Corporation."
- **"Ideal Czeki / Ideal Checks"** — the bank's budget checkbook product: 15 checks for $1.00, no minimum balance. Pitched for paying utility and household bills by mail.
- **"Bankway finansowe plany" / "BANKWAY"** — the bank's installment-buying plan; the 1945 ad introduces the "BANKWAY Credit Card" (a charge card honored by participating "Approved Dealers"). The recurring tag *"Kupujcie Na Spłaty Sposobem 'BANKWAY'"* = "Buy on installments the 'BANKWAY' way."
- **"składy"** — Buffalo Polish for retail **stores/dealers** (standard Polish: *sklepy*).
- **"kupno na spłaty / kupować na spłaty"** — to **buy on installments / on time payments**.
- **"ogonku"** — queue, line (literally "little tail").
- **"ofis"** — office (Polish-American spelling of "office").
- **"reperacja"** — in this period dialect, home **repairs** (standard modern Polish would use *naprawa/remont*).
- **"Personalcheck"** — a branded Buffalo Industrial Bank personal checking product, kept untranslated as a product name.
- Address change across the series: **Washington & Huron** (1945) → the bank **moves to Court & Pearl, "the Heart of Buffalo,"** in April 1948 — the subject of Ad 2. The Fillmore/Broadway and Cazenovia/Seneca branches persist through all ads.
