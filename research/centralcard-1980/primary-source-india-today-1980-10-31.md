# Centralcard launch — contemporaneous news coverage

## Primary source located (free, full text online)

**"Credit cards finally arrive in India, business in credit cards booms like never before"**
Chander Uday Singh, _India Today_, ISSUE DATE: **Oct 31, 1980** (republished online Dec 15, 2014)
URL: <https://www.indiatoday.in/magazine/economy/story/19801031-credit-cards-finally-arrive-in-india-business-in-credit-cards-booms-like-never-before-821571-2014-01-08>

### Key contemporaneous evidence

> "Credit cards - as American as Coca-Cola or blue jeans - have finally arrived in India. Business in credit cards is booming like never before. **Diners Club**, the pioneers, who made their entry in the early '60s now has 21,000 cardholders and 2,000 business establishments on its membership list. **Centralcard - the first indigenous credit card - which made its debut last month has already enrolled over 400 establishments on its list.**"

Image caption in piece: _"Shamsunder Aggarwal: Commission guaranteed"_ (Aggarwal was the head of Diners Club India in 1980).

### Inferred launch date
"Made its debut last month" from a 31 Oct 1980 issue → **Centralcard launched September 1980** (the IJAR/IOSR academic papers claiming "August 1980" appear to be off by about a month).
