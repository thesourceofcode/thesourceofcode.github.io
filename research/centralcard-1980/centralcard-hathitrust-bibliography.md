## Key discrepancies to resolve with primary sources

### ⭐ The "Visa network 1980" vs "Mastercard 1982" problem
Central Bank of India's own centenary history (cited by episteme.net.in PDF `Modified.pdf`) says:
> CB "became a principal member of **Mastercard in 1982** and affiliated to **VISA International in 1991**."

This **flatly contradicts** the universally-repeated modern claim that Centralcard launched "on the Visa network" in 1980.
Sources repeating Visa 1980: Mint (2025), cardinsider, cardpedia, Wikipedia, most popular articles.
Source saying Mastercard 1982: CBI's own centenary book.
→ Until someone reads the 1980 Business India / India Today India coverage found above, both claims are unverified from a primary-source standpoint.

### ⭐ Launch month
- "August 1980" — CBI centenary history (Aug is a common claim across secondary literature)
- "September 1980" — inferred from "made its debut last month" in India Today Oct 31, 1980
- The Business India **Jan–Jun 1980, p.23** hit (TIER 1 #1) may settle this definitively.
