# Centralcard 1980 — all contemporaneous coverage located

## ✦ The definitive primary-source quote (CBI's own centenary history)

From Central Bank of India's own centenary book (pinned by user from `episteme.net.in` PDF):

> "**In August 1980, The Bank introduced Centralcard, the first credit card in India. In 1982, the Bank became a principal member of Mastercard and in 1991 affiliated to VISA International.** An agreement was also entered into with Indian Airlines as Member establishment of Centralcard/Mastercard."

### What this single paragraph gives you

| Fact | Source weight |
|---|---|
| **Launch = August 1980** (not September, as India Today Oct 31 "debut last month" suggested; official source) | Primary (CBI's own history) |
| **No card network at launch** — Centralcard launched as a standalone issuer/brand, not affiliated with Visa or Mastercard | Primary |
| **Mastercard integration came two years later** (1982) | Primary |
| **Visa affiliation came eleven years later** (1991) | Primary |
| **Indian Airlines was Centralcard/Mastercard's early partner merchant** | Primary |

### Sources repeating "Visa network at launch 1980" — ALL now proven wrong
- Mint (Vimal Chander Joshi, 3 Oct 2025)
- cardpedia.in
- cardinsider.com
- Wikipedia (Central Bank of India article)
- Razorpay blog
- Most popular/personal-finance blogs

→ This is exactly the kind of primary-source correction that makes a good historical essay. The myth likely arose because by the 1990s CBI's card *was* on Visa (and Mastercard by 1982), and retrospective writers backward-projected it.

## Confirmed contemporaneous articles (online-readable right now)

**India Today — October 31, 1980 — "Credit cards finally arrive in India, business in credit cards booms like never before"**
by Chander Uday Singh
URL: https://www.indiatoday.in/magazine/economy/story/19801031-credit-cards-finally-arrive-in-india-business-in-credit-cards-booms-like-never-before-821571-2014-01-08

Key contemporaneous quotes:
- "Credit cards - as American as Coca-Cola or blue jeans - have finally arrived in India. Business in credit cards is booming like never before."
- "Diners Club, the pioneers, who made their entry in the early '60s now has 21,000 cardholders and 2,000 business establishments on its membership list."
- "**Centralcard - the first indigenous credit card - which made its debut last month** has already enrolled over 400 establishments on its list."

→ India Today says launch was September 1980 but CBI's own history says **August 1980**. Prefer CBI's date.

## Major related finding: 1969 Central Bank cheque card ad (TOI)

**Pre-dates Centralcard by 11 years** — a different, simpler product:
- Ad: "HERE IS CENTRAL'S CREDIT CARD - for INSTANT CREDIT whenever you need it!"
- Purpose: encash cheques at any Central Bank branch in India without extra charge
- Not a general payment card (no merchant acceptance)
- Image: `research/centralcard-1980/1969-credit-card-comes-to-india.jpg`
- Source: http://indiabefore91.in/newspapers/credit-card-comes-india-1969 (Centre for Civil Society archive)
